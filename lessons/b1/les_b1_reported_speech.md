## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **reported speech** | discurso indireto | *relatar o que alguém disse* |
| **direct speech** | discurso direto | *citar exatamente* |
| **say** | dizer | *relatar* |
| **tell** | contar / dizer | *relatar a alguém* |
| **ask** | perguntar | *fazer uma pergunta* |
| **report** | relatar | *informar* |
| **tense shift** | mudança de tempo verbal | *present → past* |
| **pronoun change** | mudança de pronome | *I → he/she* |
| **time expression** | expressão de tempo | *today → that day* |
| **backshift** | retrocesso verbal | *present → past* |
| **reporting verb** | verbo de relato | *say, tell, ask* |
| **quote** | citação | *palavras exatas* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Direct**: 'I **am** tired.' **Reported**: She said she **was** tired."
>
> "**Direct**: 'I **will** help.' **Reported**: He said he **would** help."
>
> "**Direct**: 'I **have finished**.' **Reported**: She said she **had finished**."
>
> "**Pronoun change**: '**I** like it.' → He said **he** liked it. '**You** are right.' → She told me **I** was right."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Reported speech** involves:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Quoting exactly |
| B | **Reporting what someone said with tense shift** |
| C | Only questions |
| D | Only commands |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "discurso indireto / relatar o que alguém disse com mudança de tempo"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the direct to its reported form:
| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| 'I am tired.' | a. She said she was tired. |
| 'I will help.' | b. He said he would help. |
| 'I have finished.' | c. She said she had finished. |
| 'I like it.' | d. He said he liked it. |
**Key:** 'I am tired.'→a, 'I will help.'→b, 'I have finished.'→c, 'I like it.'→d
**br_pt_form:** "estou cansado", "vou ajudar", "terminei", "gosto"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Reported speech (fill-in-blank)
Rewrite in **reported speech**:
"'I am happy.' → She said she ______ happy."
"'I will go.' → He said he ______ go."
"'I have seen it.' → She said she ______ seen it."
**Key:** was / would go / had seen
**br_pt_form:** "estava / iria / tinha visto"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Reporting (pares)
**Report what your partner says.**

**Example:**
> A: "I am tired."
> B: "She said she was tired."
> A: "I will help you."
> B: "He said he would help me."

**Create 6 reported speech exchanges**.

## FASE 4 — Shadow CAT [2 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (L1, adaptive)
**Backshift** in reported speech means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Moving tense forward |
| B | **Moving tense backward (present → past)** |
| C | Keeping the same tense |
| D | Changing the subject |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "backshift / mover tempo verbal para trás (presente → passado)"

### Item 2 — Grammar (L1, adaptive)
Choose the correct **reported speech**:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | She said she **is** tired. |
| B | She said she **was** tired. |
| C | She said she **will be** tired. |
| D | She said she **has been** tired. |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "Ela disse que estava cansada."
