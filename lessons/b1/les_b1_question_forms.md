## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **question form** | forma interrogativa | *estrutura de pergunta* |
| **WH-question** | pergunta com WH | *what, where, when, who, why, how* |
| **yes/no question** | pergunta sim/não | *resposta sim ou não* |
| **tag question** | tag question / pergunta de confirmação | *"..., isn't it?"* |
| **indirect question** | pergunta indireta | *"Can you tell me where...?"* |
| **embedded question** | pergunta embutida | *"I wonder where..."* |
| **question word** | palavra interrogativa | *what, where, when* |
| **auxiliary** | auxiliar | *do, does, did, is, are* |
| **inversion** | inversão | *sujeito e auxiliar trocados* |
| **polite request** | pedido educado | *"Could you...?"* |
| **choice question** | pergunta de escolha | *"Tea or coffee?"* |
| **rhetorical question** | pergunta retórica | *não espera resposta* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**WH-question**: '**Where** is the station?' **Yes/No question**: '**Is** the train on time?'"
>
> "**Tag question**: 'It's cold, **isn't it**?' 'You like coffee, **don't you**?'"
>
> "**Indirect question**: '**Can you tell me** where the station is?' '**Do you know** what time it is?'"
>
> "**Embedded question**: '**I wonder** where he is.' '**I don't know** why she left.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Tag question** is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | A question with a WH-word |
| B | **A short question at the end for confirmation** |
| C | An indirect question |
| D | A rhetorical question |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "tag question / pergunta curta no fim para confirmação"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the question type to its example:
| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **WH-question** | a. Where is the station? |
| **Yes/No question** | b. Is the train on time? |
| **Tag question** | c. It's cold, isn't it? |
| **Indirect question** | d. Can you tell me where...? |
**Key:** WH-question→a, Yes/No question→b, Tag question→c, Indirect question→d
**br_pt_form:** "pergunta WH", "pergunta sim/não", "tag question", "pergunta indireta"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Question forms (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **correct question form**:
"______ (where/the station)? ______ (the train/on time)? It's cold, ______ (isn't it)? ______ (can you tell me/where)?"
**Key:** Where is the station? / Is the train on time? / isn't it? / Can you tell me where?
**br_pt_form:** "Onde fica a estação? / O trem está no horário? / não é? / Pode me dizer onde?"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Question Forms (pares)
**Practice different question forms** about travel.

**Example:**
> A: "**Where** is the hotel?" (WH)
> B: "**Is** the hotel near the station?" (Yes/No)
> A: "The hotel is nice, **isn't it**?" (Tag)
> B: "**Can you tell me** how to get there?" (Indirect)

**Create 8 questions** using WH, Yes/No, Tag, and Indirect forms.
