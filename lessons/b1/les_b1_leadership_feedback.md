## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Feedback loop** is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | A one-time event |
| B | **A continuous process of evaluation and improvement** |
| C | A meeting |
| D | A report |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "feedback loop / processo contínuo de avaliação e melhoria"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the leadership term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Delegation** | a. Assigning tasks |
| **Empowerment** | b. Giving autonomy |
| **Accountability** | c. Taking responsibility |
| **Recognition** | d. Valuing contributions |
**Key:** Delegation→a, Empowerment→b, Accountability→c, Recognition→d
**br_pt_form:** "delegação", "empoderamento", "responsabilidade", "reconhecimento"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for leadership habits (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"She ______ (coach) juniors. He ______ (mentor) new hires. They ______ (hold) weekly one-on-ones."
**Key:** coaches / mentors / hold
**br_pt_form:** "orienta / mentora / fazem"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Leadership Scenario (pares)
**Scenario**: You are a team lead. Give feedback to a team member.

**Structure:**
1. **Recognition** (positive)
2. **Constructive feedback** (improvement area)
3. **Coaching** (how to improve)
4. **Accountability** (next steps)

**Example:**
> "Great job on the report. One area: add more data next time. I can coach you on that. Let's review again next week."
