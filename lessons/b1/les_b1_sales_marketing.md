| **lead** | lead / potencial cliente | *interessado em comprar* |
| **prospect** | prospect / candidato | *possível cliente* |

| **conversion** | conversão | *lead → cliente* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Sales**: 'We closed a big **deal** today. The **customer** is happy. Our **revenue** is up.'"
>
> "**Marketing**: 'The **campaign** launches next week. Our **target** is young professionals. The **brand** is strong.'"
>
> "**Lead/Prospect**: 'We have 50 new **leads**. 10 are qualified **prospects**. The **conversion** rate is 20%.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Lead** in sales is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | A closed deal |
| B | **A potential customer** |
| C | A marketing campaign |
| D | A brand |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "lead / potencial cliente"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the sales/marketing term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Customer** | a. Buyer |
| **Prospect** | b. Possible buyer |
| **Campaign** | c. Marketing action |
| **Conversion** | d. Lead → customer |
**Key:** Customer→a, Prospect→b, Campaign→c, Conversion→d
**br_pt_form:** "cliente", "prospect", "campanha", "conversão"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for sales facts (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The company ______ (close) many deals. The team ______ (work) hard. The campaign ______ (launch) next week."
**Key:** closes / works / launches
**br_pt_form:** "fecha / trabalha / lança"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Sales Pitch (pares)
**Scenario**: Pitch a product to a potential customer.

**Useful phrases:**
- Are you interested in...?
- Our product offers...
- The price is...
- We have a special offer.
- Can I close the deal today?

**Create a 6-line sales pitch dialogue**.
