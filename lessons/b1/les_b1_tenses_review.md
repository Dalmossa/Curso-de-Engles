## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **tense** | tempo verbal | *present, past, future* |
| **present simple** | presente simples | *hábitos, fatos* |
| **past simple** | passado simples | *ações concluídas* |
| **future** | futuro | *ações futuras* |
| **present continuous** | presente contínuo | *ação em progresso* |
| **past continuous** | passado contínuo | *ação em progresso no passado* |
| **present perfect** | presente perfeito | *ação com relevância no presente* |
| **future continuous** | futuro contínuo | *ação em progresso no futuro* |
| **time marker** | marcador temporal | *yesterday, now, tomorrow* |
| **irregular verb** | verbo irregular | *não segue regra* |
| **regular verb** | verbo regular | *segue regra* |
| **auxiliary** | auxiliar | *verbo que ajuda* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Present simple**: 'I **work** every day.' **Past simple**: 'I **worked** yesterday.' **Future**: 'I **will work** tomorrow.'"
>
> "**Present continuous**: 'I **am working** now.' **Past continuous**: 'I **was working** at 3 PM.' **Future continuous**: 'I **will be working** at 3 PM.'"
>
> "**Present perfect**: 'I **have worked** here for 5 years.' **Time markers**: yesterday, now, tomorrow."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Present perfect** is used for:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Actions with no connection to now |
| B | **Actions with relevance to the present** |
| C | Future actions |
| D | Past actions only |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "presente perfeito / ações com relevância no presente"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the tense to its time marker:
| Tense | Time marker |
|-------|-------------|
| **Present simple** | a. Every day |
| **Past simple** | b. Yesterday |
| **Future** | c. Tomorrow |
| **Present perfect** | d. Since 2020 |
**Key:** Present simple→a, Past simple→b, Future→c, Present perfect→d
**br_pt_form:** "presente simples", "passado simples", "futuro", "presente perfeito"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Tense selection (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **correct tense**:
"I ______ (work) every day. I ______ (work) yesterday. I ______ (work) tomorrow. I ______ (work) here since 2020."
**Key:** work / worked / will work / have worked
**br_pt_form:** "trabalho / trabalhei / trabalharei / tenho trabalhado"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Tense Timeline (individual)
**Describe your day** using all tenses.

**Example:**
> "**Present simple**: I wake up at 7. **Past simple**: Yesterday I woke up at 8. **Future**: Tomorrow I will wake up at 6. **Present perfect**: I have woken up early many times. **Present continuous**: Right now I am talking to you."

**Create your own timeline** with 6 sentences.
