## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Promotion** means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Leaving a job |
| B | **Moving to a higher position** |
| C | Being fired |
| D | Applying for a job |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "promotion / mover para posição superior"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the HR term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Salary** | a. Compensation |
| **Benefits** | b. Perks |
| **Resignation** | c. Voluntary departure |
| **Layoff** | d. Involuntary departure |
**Key:** Salary→a, Benefits→b, Resignation→c, Layoff→d
**br_pt_form:** "salário", "benefícios", "pedido de demissão", "demissão em massa"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for HR facts (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The company ______ (offer) good benefits. She ______ (want) a promotion. They ______ (hire) new staff."
**Key:** offers / wants / hire
**br_pt_form:** "oferece / quer / contratam"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Job Interview (pares)
**Scenario**: Practice a job interview.

**Useful phrases:**
- Tell me about yourself.
- What are your strengths?
- Why do you want this job?
- What is your expected salary?
- When can you start?

**Create a 6-line interview dialogue**.
