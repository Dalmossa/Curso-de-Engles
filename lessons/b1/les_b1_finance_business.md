### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Finance**: 'Our **revenue** is up 10%. **Expenses** are down 5%. **Profit** is growing.'"
>
> "**Budget**: 'The **budget** for Q3 is $500k. We need to cut **expenses**.'"
>
> "**Cash flow**: 'Positive **cash flow** means more money coming in than going out.'"
>
> "**Invoice/Payment**: 'Please send the **invoice**. We'll process the **payment** next week.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Revenue** is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Money going out |
| B | **Money coming in** |
| C | A budget |
| D | An expense |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "revenue / dinheiro entrando / receita"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the finance term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Budget** | a. Financial plan |
| **Profit** | b. Gain |
| **Loss** | c. Loss |
| **Investment** | d. Capital application |
**Key:** Budget→a, Profit→b, Loss→c, Investment→d
**br_pt_form:** "orçamento", "lucro", "prejuízo", "investimento"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for finance facts (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The company ______ (make) a profit. We ______ (need) to cut costs. The budget ______ (be) tight."
**Key:** makes / need / is
**br_pt_form:** "faz / precisamos / está"
