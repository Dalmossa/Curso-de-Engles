## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Cohesion** in writing refers to:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Logical flow |
| B | **Grammatical/lexical links between sentences** |
| C | Vocabulary variety |
| D | Paragraph length |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "coesão / links gramaticais entre frases"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the writing task to its focus:
| Task | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Opinion essay** | a. Argument with thesis |
| **Picture description** | b. Describe visual |
| **Business email** | c. Professional communication |
| **Conclusion** | d. Synthesize and close |
**Key:** Opinion essay→a, Picture description→b, Business email→c, Conclusion→d
**br_pt_form:** "ensaio de opinião", "descrição de imagem", "e-mail comercial", "conclusão"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Future forms for essay (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **future forms**:
"By 2030, renewable energy ______ (dominate) the market. Governments ______ (invest) heavily. Costs ______ (decrease)."
**Key:** will dominate / will invest / will decrease
**br_pt_form:** "dominará", "investirão", "diminuirão"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: TOEIC-Style Opinion Essay (individual)
**Prompt:** "Some people believe remote work increases productivity. Others disagree. What is your opinion?"

**Write 150 words:**
1. **Thesis** (1 sentence)
2. **Body 1** (claim + evidence)
3. **Body 2** (counterargument + rebuttal)
4. **Conclusion** (synthesis)

**Use:** *prompt, thesis, body paragraph, conclusion, coherence, cohesion, word count, time management*.

|------|-------|

| B | **Distributing time: plan, write, review** |
| C | Skipping tasks |
| D | Writing slowly |
**Key:** B

### Item 2 — Grammar (L1, adaptive)
Choose the correct **future form** for an essay prediction:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Renewable energy **dominates** the market by 2030. |
| B | Renewable energy **will dominate** the market by 2030. |
| C | Renewable energy **is dominating** the market by 2030. |
| D | Renewable energy **dominated** the market by 2030. |
**Key:** B
