### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> **Coach:** "**Subject line**: specific, actionable. *Decision needed: Q3 budget by Friday* — not *Budget*."
>
> **Student:** "**Salutation**: *Dear Dr. Santos,* (formal). **Opening**: context in 1 sentence. **Body**: 3 short paragraphs max. **Closing**: *Best regards,* + name + title."
>
> **Coach:** "**Conciseness**: *Please review* not *I am writing to ask you to please review*. **Action-oriented**: *Approve by EOD* not *Let me know your thoughts*. **Thread**: reply inline, trim history. **CC**: only who needs to know."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
An **action-oriented** email:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Explains background in detail |
| B | **States what the recipient should do** |
| C | Uses flowery language |
| D | Has no deadline |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "orientado à ação / diz o que o destinatário deve fazer"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the email part to its best practice:
| Part | Best Practice |
|------|---------------|
| **Subject line** | a. Specific + actionable |
| **Salutation** | b. Match formality to relationship |
| **Opening** | c. Context in 1 sentence |
| **Call to action** | d. Clear deadline + owner |
**Key:** Subject line→a, Salutation→b, Opening→c, Call to action→d
**br_pt_form:** "assunto", "saudação", "abertura", "chamada para ação"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Passive vs active for clarity (fill-in-blank)
Rewrite in **active voice**:
"The report *will be submitted* by Friday. → We ______ the report by Friday."
"The decision *was made* by the board. → The board ______ the decision."
**Key:** will submit / made
**br_pt_form:** "enviará", "tomou"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: Email Transformation (individual)
**Rewrite 3 emails for professional standards:**

1. **Subject:** Question — **Body:** Hey, just wondering if you saw my last email about the budget. Let me know.
2. **Subject:** Meeting — **Body:** Hi, I think we should meet to discuss the project. Let me know when you're free.
3. **Subject:** Update — **Body:** The project is going well. We finished the backend. Frontend is next week.

**Target:** Specific subject, proper salutation, context, action-oriented body, clear call to action, professional closing.

## FASE 4 — Shadow CAT [2 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (L1, adaptive)
**Conciseness** in professional email means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Writing long paragraphs |
| B | **Maximum meaning, minimum words** |
| C | Using big words |
| D | Writing slowly |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "concisão / máximo significado, mínimo de palavras"

### Item 2 — Grammar (L1, adaptive)
Choose the **active voice** version:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | The document *was reviewed* by legal. |
| B | **Legal *reviewed* the document.** |
| C | The document *is being reviewed* by legal. |
| D | The document *has been reviewed* by legal. |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "O jurídico revisou o documento."
