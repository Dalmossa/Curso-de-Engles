## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **business culture** | cultura de negócios | *normas e valores no ambiente corporativo* |
| **hierarchy** | hierarquia | *estrutura de poder e tomada de decisão* |
| **direct communication** | comunicação direta | *dizer o que pensa sem rodeios* |
| **indirect communication** | comunicação indireta | *sugerir sem dizer explicitamente* |
| **relationship-building** | construção de relacionamento | *investir tempo em conexões pessoais* |
| **small talk** | conversa fiápada / papo leve | *conversa informal antes de negócios* |
| **punctuality** | pontualidade | *respeito ao horário* |
| **work-life balance** | equilíbrio vida-trabalho | *fronteira entre vida pessoal e profissional* |
| **feedback culture** | cultura de feedback | *como críticas são dadas e recebidas* |
| **networking** | networking / rede de contatos | *construção de relações profissionais* |
| **formality** | formalidade | *grau de cerimônia nas interações* |
| **cultural intelligence (CQ)** | inteligência cultural | *capacidade de navegar culturas diferentes* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> **Coach:** "**BR vs US business culture**: Brazil = relationship-first, **indirect communication**, flexible time. US = task-first, **direct communication**, strict **punctuality**."
>
> "Brazil: **small talk** before meetings, **hierarchy** matters, **feedback** often softened. US: get to the point, **flat hierarchy**, **direct feedback**."
>
> "**Cultural intelligence (CQ)**: adapt without losing authenticity. **Work-life balance**: US clearer boundaries. **Networking**: both value, different styles."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Direct communication** in US business means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Hinting at problems |
| B | **Stating issues clearly and explicitly** |
| C | Avoiding conflict |
| D | Using intermediaries |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "comunicação direta / dizer claramente"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the cultural trait to the country:
| Trait | Country |
|-------|---------|
| **Relationship-first** | a. Brazil |
| **Task-first** | b. US |
| **Flexible punctuality** | c. Brazil |
| **Strict punctuality** | d. US |
**Key:** Relationship-first→a, Task-first→b, Flexible punctuality→c, Strict punctuality→d
**br_pt_form:** "relacionamento primeiro", "tarefa primeiro", "pontualidade flexível", "pontualidade rígida"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Comparatives for cultural contrast (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **comparatives**:
"Brazilian meetings tend to be ______ (long) than American ones. US feedback is often ______ (direct) than Brazilian feedback. Building trust takes ______ (long) in Brazil."
**Key:** longer / more direct / longer
**br_pt_form:** "mais longas", "mais direto", "mais tempo"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Cross-Cultural Scenario (pares)
**Student A (Brazilian):** You're in a video call with a US client. Adapt your communication.
**Student B (American):** You're meeting a Brazilian partner. Show cultural intelligence.

**Scenarios:**
1. US client wants status update — be direct, data-driven.
2. Brazilian partner invites dinner before meeting — accept, build relationship.
3. Conflict: US says "This is wrong." BR hears it as personal. Navigate.

**Use:** *business culture, hierarchy, direct/indirect communication, relationship-building, small talk, punctuality, work-life balance, feedback culture, networking, formality, cultural intelligence*.
