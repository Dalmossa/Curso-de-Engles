## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Test conditions** in simulation means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Relaxed environment |
| B | **Same time limits, format, and constraints as real exam** |
| C | Open book |
| D | Group work |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "condições de teste / mesmos limites do exame real"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the simulation component to its purpose:
| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Mock exam** | a. Full practice test |
| **Score report** | b. Detailed results by section |
| **Weakness identification** | c. Map areas to improve |
| **Study plan** | d. Preparation schedule |
**Key:** Mock exam→a, Score report→b, Weakness identification→c, Study plan→d
**br_pt_form:** "exame simulado", "relatório de pontuação", "identificação de fraquezas", "plano de estudos"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Future forms for study plan (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **future forms**:
"I ______ (take) the mock exam next week. She ______ (review) her weak areas. They ______ (improve) their scores."
**Key:** will take / will review / will improve
**br_pt_form:** "vou fazer", "vai revisar", "vão melhorar"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: Study Plan (individual)
**Create a 2-week study plan** for TOEIC/IELTS/CELPE-Bras. Include:

1. **Daily tasks** (30-60 min)
2. **Mock exam** (weekend)
3. **Review cycle** (study → practice → review)
4. **Weakness focus** (2 areas)
5. **Relaxation technique** (daily)

**Template:**
```
Week 1:
- Mon-Fri: Vocabulary (30 min) + Reading (30 min)
- Sat: Mock exam (timed)
- Sun: Review + weakness identification

Week 2:
- Mon-Fri: Listening (30 min) + Writing (30 min)
- Sat: Full simulation
- Sun: Score report + next steps
```
