## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **demonstrative** | demonstrativo | *palavra que aponta* |
| **this** | este/esta/isso | *perto, singular* |
| **that** | esse/essa/aquilo | *longe, singular* |
| **these** | estes/estas | *perto, plural* |
| **those** | esses/essas/aqueles | *longe, plural* |
| **near** | perto | *próximo ao falante* |
| **far** | longe | *distante do falante* |
| **singular** | singular | *um* |
| **plural** | plural | *mais de um* |
| **here** | aqui | *local próximo* |
| **there** | aí/lá | *local distante* |
| **point** | apontar | *gesto de indicar* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**This** is my book. **That** is your book. **These** are my pens. **Those** are your pens."
>
> "**This** is **here** (near). **That** is **there** (far). **These** are **here**. **Those** are **there**."
>
> "**This** (singular, near). **That** (singular, far). **These** (plural, near). **Those** (plural, far)."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**This** is used for:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Far, plural |
| B | **Near, singular** |
| C | Far, singular |
| D | Near, plural |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "this / perto, singular"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the demonstrative to its use:
| Demonstrative | Use |
|---------------|-----|
| **This** | a. Near, singular |
| **That** | b. Far, singular |
| **These** | c. Near, plural |
| **Those** | d. Far, plural |
**Key:** This→a, That→b, These→c, Those→d
**br_pt_form:** "este", "esse", "estes", "esses"

### Item 3 — Grammar: This/These + noun (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **this/that/these/those**:
"______ is my car (near). ______ is your house (far). ______ are my shoes (near). ______ are your books (far)."
**Key:** This / That / These / Those
**br_pt_form:** "Este / Esse / Estes / Esses"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Demonstrative Tour (pares)
**Game**: Point to objects in the room and describe them.

**Example:**
> A: "**This** is my pen. **That** is your pen."
> B: "**These** are my books. **Those** are your books."
> A: "**This** is **here**. **That** is **there**."

**Create 8 sentences** using this/that/these/those with objects in the room.
