### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**American English**: *apartment, elevator, color, center*. **British English**: *flat, lift, colour, centre*."
>
> "**Spelling**: US *color* vs UK *colour*. US *center* vs UK *centre*. US *organize* vs UK *organise*."
>
> "**Vocabulary**: US *truck* vs UK *lorry*. US *cookie* vs UK *biscuit*. US *subway* vs UK *underground*."
>
> "**Pronunciation**: US *schedule* (skedule) vs UK *schedule* (shedule). **Accent** varies by region."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Flat** in British English means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Not sharp |
| B | **Apartment** |
| C | A tire |
| D | A floor |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "flat / apartamento (UK)"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the US word to its UK equivalent:
| US | UK |
|----|----|
| **Apartment** | a. Flat |
| **Elevator** | b. Lift |
| **Truck** | c. Lorry |
| **Cookie** | d. Biscuit |
**Key:** Apartment→a, Elevator→b, Truck→c, Cookie→d
**br_pt_form:** "apartamento", "elevador", "caminhão", "biscoito"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Spelling differences (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **US or UK spelling**:
"______ (color/colour) — US. ______ (color/colour) — UK. ______ (center/centre) — US. ______ (center/centre) — UK."
**Key:** color / colour / center / centre
**br_pt_form:** "cor / centro"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Dialect Detection (pares)
**Listen and identify**: American or British English?

1. "I live in a flat near the lift." (UK)
2. "Take the elevator to the apartment." (US)
3. "The lorry carried biscuits." (UK)
4. "The truck carried cookies." (US)

**Create 4 sentences**: 2 US, 2 UK. Partner identifies the dialect.
