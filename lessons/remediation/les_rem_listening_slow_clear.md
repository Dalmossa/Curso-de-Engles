### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Reduced speed**: 'Please speak **slowly and clearly**. I need to **comprehend** every word.'"
>
> "**Replay**: 'Can you **replay** that? I didn't catch the last part.'"
>
> "**Transcript**: 'Read the **transcript** while listening. **Pause** and **replay** as needed.'"
>
> "**Native speaker**: 'Listen to a **native speaker** at reduced speed first, then normal speed.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Reduced speed** in listening practice means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Faster than normal |
| B | **Slower than normal** |
| C | Same as normal |
| D | No speed |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "reduced speed / mais lento que o normal"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the listening term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Transcript** | a. Text of the audio |
| **Replay** | b. Listen again |
| **Pause** | c. Stop the audio |
| **Playback** | d. Play the audio |
**Key:** Transcript→a, Replay→b, Pause→c, Playback→d
**br_pt_form:** "transcrição", "repetir", "pausar", "reprodução"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Imperatives for listening (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **imperative**:
"______ (listen) at reduced speed. ______ (replay) the audio. ______ (read) the transcript. ______ (pause) as needed."
**Key:** Listen / Replay / Read / Pause
**br_pt_form:** "Ouça / Repita / Leia / Pausa"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Listening Practice (individual)
**Practice listening** using the scaffold:

1. Listen at **reduced speed** (0.75x)
2. **Replay** difficult parts
3. Read the **transcript**
4. Listen at **normal speed** (1.0x)

**Create 6 sentences** describing your listening practice.
