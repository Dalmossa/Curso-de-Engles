| **police** | polícia | *autoridade* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> A: Do you have a **reservation**?
> B: Yes, for two people at 7 PM.
>
> A: Are you ready to **order**?
> B: Yes, I'll have the pasta. Can I have the **bill**, please?
> A: Of course. Here's your **receipt**. **Tip** is included.
>
> A: How much is this shirt?
> B: It's $50, but there's a 20% **discount** today.
>
> A: **Help!** Call the **police**!
> B: What's the **emergency**?
> A: Someone stole my bag!

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Bill** at a restaurant is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | A reservation |
| B | **The check / payment** |
| C | A tip |
| D | A menu |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "conta / pagamento"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the shopping term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Price** | a. Cost |
| **Discount** | b. Reduction |
| **Store** | c. Shop |
| **Receipt** | d. Proof of purchase |
**Key:** Price→a, Discount→b, Store→c, Receipt→d
**br_pt_form:** "preço", "desconto", "loja", "recibo"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present continuous for emergencies (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present continuous**:
"Someone ______ (steal) my bag! The police ______ (come). I ______ (need) help!"
**Key:** is stealing / are coming / need
**br_pt_form:** "está roubando / estão vindo / preciso"
