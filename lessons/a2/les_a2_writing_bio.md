### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Bio** (third person): 'John is a software engineer with 5 years of experience. He specializes in web development.'"
>
> "**Profile** (first person): 'I am a developer. I have experience in Python and JavaScript. My skills include problem-solving and teamwork.'"
>
> "**Concise**: 'Experienced developer. Skilled in Python, JavaScript, and cloud technologies.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Third person** in a bio uses:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | I, me, my |
| B | **He, she, his, her** |
| C | You, your |
| D | We, our |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "terceira pessoa / ele, ela, dele, dela"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the bio component to its content:
| Component | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Experience** | a. Work history |
| **Education** | b. Academic background |
| **Skills** | c. Competencies |
| **Achievement** | d. Positive result |
**Key:** Experience→a, Education→b, Skills→c, Achievement→d
**br_pt_form:** "experiência", "educação", "habilidades", "conquista"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Third person bio (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **third person**:
"______ (She/work) as a developer. ______ (She/have) 5 years of experience. ______ (She/specialize) in Python."
**Key:** She works / She has / She specializes
**br_pt_form:** "Ela trabalha / Ela tem / Ela se especializa"

---

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: Professional Bio (individual)
**Write a 50-word bio** in third person.

**Template:**
> "______ is a ______ with ______ years of experience. He/She specializes in ______. His/Her skills include ______. He/She is passionate about ______."

**Example:**
> "Ana is a software engineer with 5 years of experience. She specializes in web development. Her skills include Python, JavaScript, and cloud technologies. She is passionate about building user-friendly applications."
