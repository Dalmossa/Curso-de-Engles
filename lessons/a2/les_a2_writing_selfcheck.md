### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Revision checklist**: 1) **Grammar** correct? 2) **Spelling** correct? 3) **Punctuation** correct? 4) **Clarity** — easy to understand? 5) **Coherence** — logical flow? 6) **Conciseness** — no unnecessary words?"
>
> "**Proofread** your **draft**. **Edit** for clarity. Create a **final version**."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Proofread** means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Write a draft |
| B | **Read to find and correct errors** |
| C | Delete the text |
| D | Publish immediately |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "revisar / ler para encontrar e corrigir erros"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the revision step to its focus:
| Step | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Grammar** | a. Language rules |
| **Spelling** | b. Correct writing |
| **Punctuation** | c. Commas, periods |
| **Clarity** | d. Easy to understand |
**Key:** Grammar→a, Spelling→b, Punctuation→c, Clarity→d
**br_pt_form:** "gramática", "ortografia", "pontuação", "clareza"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Imperatives for revision (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **imperative**:
"______ (check) grammar. ______ (correct) spelling. ______ (edit) for clarity. ______ (proofread) the draft."
**Key:** Check / Correct / Edit / Proofread
**br_pt_form:** "Verifique / Corrija / Edite / Revise"

---

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: Revision Practice (individual)
**Write 3 sentences with errors**, then revise them.

**Example (with errors):**
> "She go to the store. He don't like coffee. They is happy."

**Revised:**
> "She **goes** to the store. He **doesn't** like coffee. They **are** happy."

**Create your own 3 sentences with errors**, then revise them.
