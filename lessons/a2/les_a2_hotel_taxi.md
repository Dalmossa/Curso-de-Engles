### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> A: I have a **reservation** at the Grand Hotel.
> B: Here's your **room key**. **Check-out** is at 11 AM.
>
> A: Where can I catch a **taxi**?
> B: Outside the terminal. The **fare** is about $20.
>
> A: What time is the next **bus**?
> B: Every 15 minutes. The **schedule** is on the app.

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Check-out** at a hotel is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Arriving |
| B | **Leaving / settling the bill** |
| C | Ordering room service |
| D | Making a reservation |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "check-out / saída / conta"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the transport term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Taxi** | a. Private car for hire |
| **Bus** | b. Public road transport |
| **Train** | c. Rail transport |
| **Subway** | d. Underground rail |
**Key:** Taxi→a, Bus→b, Train→c, Subway→d
**br_pt_form:** "táxi", "ônibus", "trem", "metrô"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for schedules (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The train ______ (leave) at 8 AM. The bus ______ (come) every 10 minutes. The subway ______ (run) 24/7."
**Key:** leaves / comes / runs
**br_pt_form:** "o trem sai / o ônibus vem / o metrô funciona"

**Useful phrases:**

- My name is...
