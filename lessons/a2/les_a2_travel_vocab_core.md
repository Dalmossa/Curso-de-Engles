### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "I have a **reservation** at the hotel. My **flight** is at 9 AM. I need to **check in** online."
>
> "My **luggage** is heavy. Where is the **baggage claim**? My **boarding pass** is on my phone."
>
> "The **departure** is delayed. The **arrival** is at 3 PM. Is my **ticket** confirmed?"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Boarding pass** is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | A hotel key |
| B | **A document to board a plane** |
| C | A train ticket |
| D | A passport |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "cartão de embarque / documento para embarcar"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the travel term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Passport** | a. Travel document |
| **Luggage** | b. Bags/suitcases |
| **Reservation** | c. Booking |
| **Departure** | d. Leaving |
**Key:** Passport→a, Luggage→b, Reservation→c, Departure→d
**br_pt_form:** "passaporte", "bagagem", "reserva", "partida"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for travel facts (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The flight ______ (leave) at 9 AM. The hotel ______ (have) a pool. The train ______ (arrive) at 3 PM."
**Key:** leaves / has / arrives
**br_pt_form:** "o voo sai / o hotel tem / o trem chega"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Travel Planning (pares)
**Plan a trip** using travel vocabulary.

**Example:**
> A: "I have a reservation at the hotel. My flight leaves at 9 AM."
> B: "Is your luggage ready? Do you have your boarding pass?"
> A: "Yes, my boarding pass is on my phone. The departure is on time."

**Create your own travel dialogue** with 6 sentences.
