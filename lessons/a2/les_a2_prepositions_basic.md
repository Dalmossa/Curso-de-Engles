## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **preposition** | preposição | *in, on, at, under, over* |
| **in** | em / dentro | *lugar fechado, período longo* |
| **on** | em / sobre | *superfície, dia específico* |
| **at** | em / no / na | *ponto específico, hora* |
| **under** | sob / debaixo de | *abaixo* |
| **over** | sobre / acima de | *acima (sem contacto)* |
| **next to** | ao lado de | *proximidade* |
| **between** | entre | *dois pontos* |
| **behind** | atrás de | *parte posterior* |
| **in front of** | na frente de | *parte anterior* |
| **by** | perto de / por | *proximidade, agente* |
| **Monday** | segunda-feira | *dia da semana* |
| **January** | janeiro | *mês* |
| **2024** | 2024 | *ano* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Place**: The book is **on** the table. The cat is **under** the chair. The bank is **next to** the post office. The park is **between** the school and the library."
>
> "**Time**: **In** January. **On** Monday. **At** 3 PM. **In** 2024. **At** night. **On** Friday morning."
>
> "**In** (long periods): in January, in 2024, in the morning. **On** (specific days): on Monday, on Friday morning. **At** (specific times): at 3 PM, at night, at noon."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**On** is used for:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Long periods |
| B | **Surfaces and specific days** |
| C | Specific times |
| D | All of the above |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "on / superfícies e dias específicos"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the preposition to its use:
| Preposition | Use |
|-------------|-----|
| **In** | a. Long periods (January, 2024) |
| **On** | b. Surfaces and days (on Monday) |
| **At** | c. Specific times (at 3 PM) |
| **Under** | d. Below (under the table) |
**Key:** In→a, On→b, At→c, Under→d
**br_pt_form:** "em", "sobre/em", "no/na", "debaixo de"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Prepositions (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **in/on/at**:
"______ January. ______ Monday. ______ 3 PM. ______ 2024. ______ night. ______ Friday morning."
**Key:** In / On / At / In / At / On
**br_pt_form:** "Em / Na / Às / Em / À / Na"

**Example:**
> "My desk is **in** the corner. My computer is **on** the desk. The printer is **next to** the window. The whiteboard is **behind** the door. The plant is **between** the desk and the shelf."

**Create your own description** with 6 sentences.
