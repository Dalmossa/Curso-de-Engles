| **meanwhile** | enquanto isso | *paralelamente* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Present continuous** for actions happening **right now**: 'I **am working**.' 'She **is meeting** a client.' 'They **are developing** a new feature.'"
>
> "**At the moment / Currently**: 'We **are currently** hiring.' 'He **is temporarily** working from home.'"
>
> "**While**: '**While** I **am writing**, she **is testing**.' **Meanwhile**, the team **is deploying**."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Present continuous** is used for:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Past actions |
| B | **Actions happening now** |
| C | Future plans |
| D | General facts |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "presente contínuo / ações acontecendo agora"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the time expression to its use:
| Expression | Use |
|------------|-----|
| **Right now** | a. This exact moment |
| **At the moment** | b. Current period |
| **Currently** | c. Present time |
| **Meanwhile** | d. At the same time (parallel) |
**Key:** Right now→a, At the moment→b, Currently→c, Meanwhile→d
**br_pt_form:** "agora mesmo", "no momento", "atualmente", "enquanto isso"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present continuous (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **am/is/are + -ing**:
"I ______ (work). She ______ (meet). They ______ (develop). We ______ (test)."
**Key:** am working / is meeting / are developing / are testing
**br_pt_form:** "estou trabalhando / está encontrando / estão desenvolvendo / estão testando"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Workflow Description (individual)
**Describe what you and your team are doing right now.**

**Example:**
> "**Right now**, I **am writing** this document. **Meanwhile**, my colleague **is reviewing** the code. **At the moment**, we **are working** on the new feature. **Currently**, the team **is preparing** the release."

**Create your own description** with 5 sentences.
