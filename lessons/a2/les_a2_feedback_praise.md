### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> A: **Great job** on the presentation! **Well done**!
> B: Thank you. I worked hard on it.
> A: One piece of **feedback**: speak a bit slower next time.
> B: Good point. I'll **improve** that.
> A: Also, I'm **sorry** for the late reply to your email.
> B: **No problem**. **Keep it up**!

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Constructive feedback** is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Only negative |
| B | **Helpful and actionable** |
| C | Only positive |
| D | Vague |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "feedback construtivo / útil e acionável"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the phrase to its use:
| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Well done** | a. Praise |
| **I'm sorry** | b. Apology |
| **My fault** | c. Taking responsibility |
| **Keep it up** | d. Encouragement |
**Key:** Well done→a, I'm sorry→b, My fault→c, Keep it up→d
**br_pt_form:** "bem feito", "sinto muito", "minha culpa", "continue assim"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Past simple for feedback (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **past simple**:
"You ______ (do) a great job. She ______ (work) hard. They ______ (improve) a lot."
**Key:** did / worked / improved
**br_pt_form:** "fez / trabalhou / melhoraram"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Feedback Session (pares)
**Scenario**: Give feedback to a colleague.

**Structure:**
1. **Praise** (1 positive thing)
2. **Constructive feedback** (1 improvement area)
3. **Encouragement** (keep it up)

**Example:**
> A: "You did a great job on the report. One thing: add more data next time. Keep it up!"
> B: "Thanks. I'll improve that."

**Create your own feedback dialogue**.
