> "A: Where is **immigration**?"
> B: "Go through **security** first, then **baggage claim**."
>
> A: "Do I need to fill out a **customs declaration**?"
> B: "Yes. What's your **purpose of visit**?"
> A: "Tourism. **Duration of stay**: two weeks."
> B: "Here's your **boarding pass**. **Gate** B12."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Immigration** at the airport is:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | The baggage claim area |
| B | **Passport control / entry check** |
| C | The customs area |
| D | The security check |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "imigração / controle de passaporte / entrada"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the airport term to its meaning:
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Gate** | a. Boarding area |
| **Terminal** | b. Airport building |
| **Carry-on** | c. Bag you take on board |
| **Checked bag** | d. Bag in cargo hold |
**Key:** Gate→a, Terminal→b, Carry-on→c, Checked bag→d
**br_pt_form:** "portão de embarque", "terminal", "bagagem de mão", "bagagem despachada"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Present simple for airport procedures (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **present simple**:
"The flight ______ (depart) at 10 AM. Passengers ______ (go) through security. The gate ______ (close) 20 minutes before departure."
**Key:** departs / go / closes
**br_pt_form:** "o voo parte / passageiros passam / o portão fecha"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Airport Roleplay (pares)
**Scenario**: You are at immigration. The officer asks you questions.

**Useful phrases:**
- Purpose of visit: tourism/business
- Duration of stay: X days/weeks
- Where are you staying: hotel/friend's place
- Do you have anything to declare: no

**Create a 6-line dialogue** with the immigration officer.
