### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**First**, I arrived at the office. **Then**, I checked my emails. **After that**, I attended a meeting. **Finally**, I finished the report."
>
> "**Yesterday**, I worked from home. **Last week**, we launched the project. Two days **ago**, I met the client."
>
> "**While** I was working, the server crashed. **Suddenly**, everyone stopped. **Meanwhile**, the team fixed the issue."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Then** is used to:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Show contrast |
| B | **Show sequence (next)** |
| C | Show cause |
| D | Show time |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "depois / então / mostrar sequência"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the sequence marker to its use:
| Marker | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **First** | a. Beginning |
| **Then** | b. Next step |
| **After that** | c. Following step |
| **Finally** | d. Conclusion |
**Key:** First→a, Then→b, After that→c, Finally→d
**br_pt_form:** "primeiro", "depois", "depois disso", "finalmente"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Past simple + sequence (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **past simple + sequence marker**:
"______ (first/arrive). ______ (then/check) emails. ______ (after that/attend) a meeting. ______ (finally/finish)."
**Key:** First I arrived / Then I checked / After that I attended / Finally I finished
**br_pt_form:** "Primeiro cheguei / Depois verifiquei / Depois disso participei / Finalmente terminei"

---

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Yesterday at Work (individual)
**Narrate your yesterday** using past simple + sequence markers.

**Example:**
> "**First**, I woke up at 7. **Then**, I had breakfast. **After that**, I went to work. **While** I was working, I had a meeting. **Suddenly**, the fire alarm rang. **Finally**, I finished at 6 PM."

**Create your own narrative** with 6 sentences.
