> "**Collocations**: '**make** a reservation' (not *do*). '**book** a ticket' (not *order*). '**catch** a flight' (not *take* in this context)."

> "'**Heavy** traffic' (not *strong*). '**Strong** coffee' (not *heavy*). '**Fast** food' (not *quick* in this context)."
>
> "'**Check-in** desk'. '**Baggage** claim'. '**Duty-free** shop'."

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Make a reservation** is correct because:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | "Make" collocates with "reservation" |
| B | **"Make" collocates with "reservation"** |
| C | "Do" is always correct |
| D | Any verb works |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "make / coloca com reservation"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the collocation to its meaning:
| Collocation | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Catch a flight** | a. Board a plane |
| **Miss a flight** | b. Fail to board |
| **Take a taxi** | c. Use a cab |
| **Heavy traffic** | d. Congested roads |
**Key:** Catch a flight→a, Miss a flight→b, Take a taxi→c, Heavy traffic→d
**br_pt_form:** "pegar um voo", "perder um voo", "pegar um táxi", "trânsito pesado"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Collocations in sentences (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **correct collocation**:
"I ______ (make/do) a reservation. She ______ (catch/take) a flight. They ______ (have/get) heavy traffic."
**Key:** make / catch / have
**br_pt_form:** "faço / pega / têm"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Travel Collocations (pares)
**Create sentences** using 8 travel collocations.

**Example:**
> "I **made a reservation** at the hotel. We **caught a flight** to Paris. There was **heavy traffic** on the way. I drank **strong coffee** to stay awake. We shopped at the **duty-free** store."

**Create your own travel story** with 8 collocations.
