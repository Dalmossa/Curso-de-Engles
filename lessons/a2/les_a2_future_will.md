## FASE 1 — Input (i+1) [4 min]

### Vocabulário-chave (com gloss PT-BR)

| English | PT-BR | Contexto |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **will** | vai | *futuro espontâneo, promessas* |
| **going to** | vai (planeado) | *futuro planeado, intenções* |
| **I will** | eu vou | *decisão no momento* |
| **I am going to** | eu vou (planeado) | *intenção prévia* |
| **prediction** | previsão | *futuro incerto* |
| **promise** | promessa | *compromisso* |
| **decision** | decisão | *escolha* |
| **plan** | plano | *intenção organizada* |
| **probably** | provavelmente | *possibilidade* |
| **maybe** | talvez | *incerteza* |
| **soon** | em breve | *futuro próximo* |
| **tomorrow** | amanhã | *dia seguinte* |
| **next week** | próxima semana | *semana seguinte* |

### Mini-texto (áudio: `deferred_render`)

> "**Will**: spontaneous decisions and promises. 'I **will** help you.' 'It **will** rain tomorrow.' (prediction)"
>
> "**Going to**: plans and intentions. 'I **am going to** visit Paris next month.' 'She **is going to** start a new job.'"
>
> "**Probably/Maybe**: 'It will **probably** rain.' 'Maybe I **will** go.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Will** is used for:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Past actions |
| B | **Spontaneous decisions, promises, predictions** |
| C | Present habits |
| D | Plans made long ago |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "will / decisões espontâneas, promessas, previsões"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the future form to its use:
| Form | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Will** | a. Spontaneous decision / promise |
| **Going to** | b. Plan / intention |
| **Probably** | c. Likely prediction |
| **Maybe** | d. Uncertain possibility |
**Key:** Will→a, Going to→b, Probably→c, Maybe→d
**br_pt_form:** "will", "going to", "provavelmente", "talvez"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Will vs Going to (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **will / going to**:
"I ______ (help) you now. (spontaneous) She ______ (visit) Paris next month. (plan) It ______ (rain) tomorrow. (prediction)"
**Key:** will help / is going to visit / will rain
**br_pt_form:** "vou ajudar / vai visitar / vai chover"

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Speaking: Future Plans (pares)
**Ask and answer** about future plans:

1. What will you do tomorrow?
2. What are you going to do next weekend?
3. Will you travel next year?
4. What are your plans for next month?
5. Maybe you will...?

**Example:**
> A: "What are you going to do next weekend?"
> B: "I am going to visit my family."
> A: "Will you travel next year?"
> B: "Maybe I will go to Japan."
