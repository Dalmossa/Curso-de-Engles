> "**Narrating the past**: use **past tense** and **chronological order**. '**First**, I arrived. **Then**, I worked. **Finally**, I left.'"

## FASE 2 — Guided Practice [5 min]

### Item 1 — Vocabulary (MCQ)
**Chronological** order means:
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | Random |
| B | **Time sequence** |
| C | Alphabetical |
| D | Reverse |
**Key:** B
**br_pt_form:** "cronológico / sequência temporal"

### Item 2 — Vocabulary (Matching)
Match the narrative marker to its use:
| Marker | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Meanwhile** | a. Parallel action |
| **Suddenly** | b. Unexpected event |
| **Eventually** | c. Final outcome |
| **Afterward** | d. Later action |
**Key:** Meanwhile→a, Suddenly→b, Eventually→c, Afterward→d
**br_pt_form:** "enquanto isso", "de repente", "eventualmente", "depois"

### Item 3 — Grammar: Past tense narrative (fill-in-blank)
Complete with **past tense**:
"I ______ (arrive) at 9. I ______ (work) all day. I ______ (finish) at 5. I ______ (go) home."
**Key:** arrived / worked / finished / went
**br_pt_form:** "cheguei / trabalhei / terminei / fui"

---

## FASE 3 — Output Task [4 min]

### Writing: Past Narrative (individual)
**Write a 6-sentence narrative** about your last workday.

**Template:**
> "**First**, I ______. **Then**, I ______. **Meanwhile**, my colleague ______. **Suddenly**, ______. **Eventually**, we ______. **Afterward**, I ______."

**Example:**
> "**First**, I arrived at 9. **Then**, I checked emails. **Meanwhile**, my colleague was in a meeting. **Suddenly**, the server crashed. **Eventually**, we fixed it. **Afterward**, I went home."
