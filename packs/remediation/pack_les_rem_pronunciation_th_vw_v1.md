### ITEM 6
**item_id:** `grammar.articles_sounds_002`
**domain:** `grammar`
**level:** L1
**stem:** You mean the **sound /θ/** — the *th* in *think*, not the letter T-H. Which article comes
before it?
| Slot | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | **a** |
| B | an |
| C | the |
| D | no article — sounds never take one |
**Key:** A
**br_pt_form:** "o som /θ/ é consoante → **a** th sound (não *an*, apesar do 'th')"

> **Rewritten 2026-10-01.** The old stem was ambiguous in the same way as its twin in
> `pack_les_a1_pronunciation_intro_v1.md` — *"Choose the correct article before a sound"* — and the
> two carried opposite keys, so the corpus taught both answers to the same question. This one now
> names the *sound value* /θ/ explicitly, which makes `a` correct by rule and puts it in direct
> contrast with the letter-name item beside it. Found by [11c].
